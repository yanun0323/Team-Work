## What's new

**Use your other Mac from here (Power Tower)**
There's a new Power Tower on the map. Pair two computers that both run Team Work once, and you can jump over to the other one's island and work its companies, employees, tasks, chats and terminals as if you were sitting in front of it. The work runs on that computer, with its own models and settings, so it keeps going even after you close this one. Pairing only happens when the other computer says yes, and each computer has its own switch for who's allowed in. The other computer does need to keep Team Work open.

**Delete a blueprint even if people use it**
Before, you couldn't delete a blueprint while any employee was built from it. Now you pick another blueprint, everyone moves over to it (names, looks, models and memories stay the same), and the old one is gone.

**Employees write fewer documents**
Unless you ask for one, employees no longer create spec, plan or report files. They put that in their handoff instead, and still fix existing docs their changes made wrong.

**Two new built-in skills**
Employees can now use a commit skill and a skill for drawing pictures with block characters in the terminal.

## Fixes

- In chats, employees sometimes slipped into English after reading lots of English output. They now get a reminder of your language every turn.
