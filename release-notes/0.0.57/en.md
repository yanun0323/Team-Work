## What's new

**Experimental: search by meaning**
Employees can now look through a project by what the code means, not just the words in it. Ask for "where we save the user's settings" and it finds the right files even if they never use those words. It runs on your Mac's Neural Engine, so nothing leaves your computer. It's off by default: turn it on in Settings, then restart the app (the app can do the restart for you). It needs macOS 27 on Apple silicon, and the switch only shows up on Macs that can run it. The first time, it downloads the model and reads through your project; until that's done, or if anything goes wrong, search quietly falls back to plain keyword search.

**The staffing agency is now the Immigration Office**
Same place, same job, new name.

**Cleaner hover on the island**
Pointing at a company, the airport, the power tower or the Immigration Office now darkens just the building's own shape, without darker patches where parts overlap. The whole lot of a company now responds to the pointer, including the ground under the overhang.
