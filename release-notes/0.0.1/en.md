The first release of Team Work.

Team Work lets a team of AI helpers work on your own projects. Each project is a company in a building on a little pixel island. You give the company a task, the helpers work through it step by step, and you check the result before it goes in.

- Works on macOS 12 or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- Signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without warnings.
- From now on, the app updates itself when a new version is out.
