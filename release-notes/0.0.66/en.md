## What's new

**Company memory**
Memory now comes in two kinds. Company memory holds a project's conventions, commands and pitfalls, and everyone who works in that company shares it, including people borrowed from the Immigration Office. Each note shows who wrote it. An employee's own memory holds their way of working and goes with them to any company. Employees save notes into company memory unless they say otherwise.

**A Memory tab for each company**
Company memory has its own tab in the company file. Click a note to see who wrote it, when, and in which chat. You can add notes, correct them or remove them there.

**Keep people when you disband a company**
When you disband a company, you can tick the employees you want to keep. They move to the Immigration Office with their own memory, and everyone else leaves with the company.

**Moving keeps an employee's memory**
Moving an employee between a company and the Immigration Office no longer wipes their own notes.

## Fixes

**Riding out a short provider outage**
When a model provider stops answering for a moment, replies now keep retrying for about a minute instead of a few seconds. A provider that's down for 20 seconds no longer fails the reply.

**Companies staffed only from the Immigration Office**
A company whose only staff come from the Immigration Office can now open chats and tasks. Before, it said there were no employees.

**Send button needs one click**
A hint under message boxes used to disappear the moment you clicked away, moving the send button so it took two clicks. The hint is gone, so one click sends.
