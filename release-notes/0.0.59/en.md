## What's new

**The power tower crackles when it opens**
Opening the power tower now plays a short buzz of electric current. It's under the same Opening buildings switch as the other building sounds in Settings.

**Writing to a stopped owner gets them going again**
When a task's owner stops because it hit the limit on back-and-forth questions, send-backs or failed runs, you no longer have to press Let the owner go on first: just write to them and they pick up from your message. If they stopped at the cost limit, you still answer the budget question first.

## Fixes

- The app no longer fails to start when a provider stops offering a model one of your employees uses, for example after you change its address or key. The employee keeps its settings until you pick another model.
