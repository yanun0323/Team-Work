## What's new

**Long chats are summed up at the right time**
When a conversation gets close to its limit, Team Work now asks the model's provider how long it really is, instead of only guessing. Chats are summed up when they need to be, not too early or too late, and the usage you see matches what was counted. This works with Anthropic, OpenAI and most self-hosted servers.

**Shorter instructions, fewer tokens**
The list of tools and skills sent with each reply is shorter now, so every reply costs a little less.

## Fixes

**No more silent stops**
If a model ends its turn without saying anything, Team Work now asks it once more instead of stopping there.
