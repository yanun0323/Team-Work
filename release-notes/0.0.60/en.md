## What's new

**Closing the window no longer quits the app**
Closing the window just puts it away. Your companies keep working, and notifications and the Dock badge keep coming. Click the Dock icon to get the window back, or use Minimize (⌘M) and Close Window (⌘W) from the new Window menu. To quit for real, use ⌘Q.

**Team Work lives in the menu bar**
While the window is closed, the Dock icon goes away and a small icon in the menu bar shows how many things are waiting for you. From there you can open the window, check for updates or quit. Opening the window again, from the menu bar, Finder or a notification, brings the Dock icon back.

## Fixes

- Chats no longer jump around while you scroll back through them. When new messages come in or a reply finishes, what you're reading stays put.
- Gemini 3 models no longer fail with an error after using a tool.
