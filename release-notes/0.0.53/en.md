## What's new

**A staffing agency for the whole island**
A domed building now stands just past the airport. Employees hired there are shared: every company can pick them, and they work with that company's folder, rules and permissions. You can move an employee from a company into the agency, or from the agency into a company. A move starts them fresh, keeping their name, look and settings but not their memory. Townsfolk now walk in and out of the agency's door, and the paths around it stay clear.

**Copy employees from another computer**
When you look at a paired computer's island, the copy button on an employee's details copies them into your own agency. You choose whether to bring only their skill sources or the skill files too, and you pick a model first if your computer doesn't have theirs.

**Copy an employee in one click**
The copy button on an employee's details opens a new hire form already filled in with their blueprint, model, thinking level and look. The name gets a "-1" at the end, and nothing is hired until you press Add.

**Meetings flow more like a real conversation**
In a meeting, people can now hand the floor to someone else by writing @name. If nobody is named, the turn goes back to the chair. You can jump in at any time: what you say replaces the turns still waiting, and mentioning an employee who is allowed on the task brings them into the meeting. Speaking to a meeting that paused at its limit starts it up again.

**A smoother island and faster chat lists**
The island takes much less work to draw, so it moves more smoothly, especially with many people around. Long chat lists open faster too.

## Fixes

- When a merge fails, the task now says why, for example uncommitted files in the project folder that the merge would overwrite, and what to do next. The reason shows above the reply box, in the conversation and on the merge row, and the Merge button can't be pressed twice.
- Labels on the map no longer show on top of the panels over it.
- Copying an employee from another computer keeps their thinking level, and sending the same copy again no longer hires them twice, even after a restart.
