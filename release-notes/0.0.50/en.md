## What's new

**Change a running task's flow**
You can now reshape the flow of a task that is already under way, from its edit sheet: add steps, remove ones that haven't run yet, or rearrange them. Steps that already ran are shown faded and stay put, and each change is listed in the task's conversation.

**The owner can talk to a step while it works**
A task's owner can now send a note to an employee who is in the middle of a step, without stopping them. They read it as soon as they finish what they're doing. If they finish first, the conversation tells you the note didn't reach them.

**Sending work back is gentler**
When the owner sends a step back while later steps are still working on top of it, those steps are asked to wrap up and hand in what they found first. If that takes more than 5 minutes, you're asked whether to stop them now or wait. The redo also starts with the reason and what was handed in last time, so it fixes what was wrong instead of starting over.

**The owner can open new tasks and ask you to merge**
When you ask for work that falls outside the task, the owner can put it into a new task, which waits in To do and opens from its line in the conversation. When the work is done, the owner can ask you to merge with a card you answer in one click.

**Names instead of codes**
When the owner mentions an employee, a step's run or one of its saved notes, the conversation now shows the employee's face and name, or a short preview, instead of an internal code. Click it to open it.

**Copy a message**
Each message in a task's conversation has a copy button.

**The airport staff can use every skill**
The staff at the airport can now use the skills from every blueprint and source, not just their own.

**Lower model costs**
Employees reuse cached prompts much more often, so long tasks cost less and respond faster.

## Fixes

- Pressing Esc with the hand-off menu open closes just the menu, not the whole chat.
- Opening a saved note the owner mentioned and pressing Esc closes only that note.
- Usage is still counted when a model call fails or is canceled.
- Very long conversations full of saved reasoning can be summed up again.
- The sea foam lines on the map fade in smoothly.
