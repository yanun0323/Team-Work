## What's new

**Make a one-off flow for a task**
- When you create a task, switch Flow to Custom and lay out the steps right there.
- Start from a blank step, or import a workflow and change it.
- Click a step to edit it. The flow belongs to that task only. Press Save as workflow if you want to reuse it.

**Tasks show up in the chat**
- A task made from a chat now appears as a card under the message it came from.
- Click the card to open the task. It stays there when you open the chat again.

**Skills in meetings**
- In a meeting, typing `$` lists the skills of everyone in the chat, not just one person's.
- Everyone who replies that round gets the same skill.

**Fewer, clearer built-in blueprints**
- Full-Stack Engineer is now part of Generalist. Employees using it move over on their own.
- New: Matt Pocock and Poteto, who work with skills their authors publish on GitHub.

**Smaller fixes**
- Long drop-down lists open upward when there's no room below.
- Hover above a bar in the daily chart to see that day's total.
- Usage limits refresh more often, without asking the provider every time.
