## What's new

**Hide the answer buttons in a chat**
When someone asks you a question with a long list of answers, the buttons can take up a lot of room. Now you can click the line with their name to tuck the buttons away and read the whole conversation. Click it again to bring them back. You can still type your answer while they're hidden, and the next question shows its buttons again on its own.

**Open a skill source on GitHub**
If a skill source comes from GitHub, its page now has a button that takes you straight to that repo, on the right branch.
