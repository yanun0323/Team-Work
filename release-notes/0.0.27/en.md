## What's new

**Buildings grow at a calmer pace**
Your company's building used to shoot up to its top floor even for an ordinary project. Now it gets a second floor once the project hits 1 MB, then one more floor every time the project gets about 2.5 times bigger. You'll only see all ten floors on really big projects, around 1.5 GB.

## Fixes

**Questions in chat read like normal messages again**
When an employee needs you to pick something, they now ask the question in their reply like they normally would, and the choices show up right under it. Before, the question could end up in a separate box, and sometimes the employee used that to dodge actually answering you.
