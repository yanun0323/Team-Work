## What's new

**Day and night on the island**
- The island now has a time of day. At night, the windows of companies that are working light up.
- Pick how time moves in Settings → **Time of day**: real clock time, Minecraft (a full day in 20 minutes, the default), always day, or always night.

**Workflows are more flexible**
- A workflow no longer has to begin with Clarify requirements and end with Acceptance review. Any step with no arrow coming in is a starting point, and they all begin together when the task starts.
- A task is ready to merge once every branch that actually started is done.
- Each step can now turn **Can send back** on or off, so you decide which steps may send work back to be redone.
- A work step has one employee. A discussion step can have several, which turns it into a meeting: the first one chairs, and the ones you @ answer in turn.
- Your existing workflows and tasks keep working as before.

**Employees without a blueprint**
- You can give an employee their own custom settings (prompt, skills and tools) instead of a blueprint.
- Start from scratch, or press **Import from blueprint** to copy one and tweak it. The copy no longer follows the blueprint.
- Custom settings belong to that employee only and go away when they leave.

**New tasks wait for you**
- A new task now sits in To do until you press **Start**. This also goes for tasks your employees create.

**Settings**
- "Waiting page first" is now **Auto-switch default page**: off, chats first, or tasks first.

**Small changes**
- In Chinese, workflows are now called 任務藍圖.
- The money tossed off a busy building looks more like real bills.
