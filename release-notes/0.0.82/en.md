## What's new

**Open a friend's company from your own island**
When you add a new company, you can now pick one of a paired friend's companies instead of a project folder. It stands on your island in cool blue with a mast on the roof, and you open it right there without flying over. The work still runs on your friend's Mac. If they only let you view, you can look but not change anything; if their Mac can't be reached, the building turns grey. Chats waiting for your reply there count in the number on the Dock. Removing it only takes down your copy, never your friend's company.
