## What's new

**Choose the picture size**
When an employee draws a picture, they can now ask for an exact size, such as a wide 2048×1152. With a ChatGPT subscription it goes up to 3840×2160; with your own image server, the server decides how large it can go. Leave it out and you get the usual size.

## Fixes

**Large pictures from your own image server finish**
A big picture from a self-hosted image server used to fail after two minutes, or when the file was large. Team Work now waits up to 15 minutes and takes much larger files.

**Very large pictures still show in the chat**
A picture too large to attach to the chat now shows as a smaller preview, instead of not showing at all. The full picture is still saved in your folder.
