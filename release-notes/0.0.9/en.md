## What's new

**Company notes**
- Every company file now has a yellow sticky note in the top right corner. It shows the first few lines of your notes.
- Click it to open the notes. Click the text to edit it. Your notes save as you type.
- Notes are saved in your project folder at `.team-work/NOTES.md`, so they go wherever the project goes. They're for you: employees aren't handed them, and they aren't committed automatically.

**Nicer nights**
- When you open an office or the airport after dark, the rooms inside light up with soft, even light.
- Street lamps, the airport's big glass front and the control tower now glow at night.
- The sea at dawn and dusk has softer purple-blue colors.

**Clearer names**
- "AGENTS.md" in the app is now called **Global employee rules** (in Airport → Settings) and **Company employee rules** (in a company's settings). They're still the same AGENTS.md files.

**Small changes**
- To add someone to a meeting, @ mention them. The **Invite others** button is gone.
- The **Tutorial** section moved lower on the Settings page.
