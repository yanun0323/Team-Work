## What's new

**A plane brings you in.** When the app opens, you now see open sea first, then follow a plane as it lands on your island while the buttons slide into place. Hopping over to another computer's island (or coming back) works the same way: a plane takes off from where you are and lands where you're going. If you've turned on "reduce motion" on your Mac, clouds simply roll over the map instead.

**The power tower is now a cell tower.** It looks like one and is called one everywhere in the app and in Help. It's also bigger, and the helipad moved over so the two don't bump into each other. In its panel, the "Go" button now sits right on each computer's first line, and adding a new computer starts by asking which side this one plays, so the setup is easier to follow.

**The "which island am I on" note gets out of the way.** It now sits flat in the bottom corner, and when you open a panel it folds down into a small square with an arrow so it doesn't cover what you're reading.

## Fixes

- If you put a tall company right at either end of the airport runway, planes used to fly straight through it. They now climb over it, landing a bit later or taking off a bit sooner when they need to.
