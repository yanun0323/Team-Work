## What's new

**Blueprints and workflows now come from one shared place**
The 13 starter blueprints (Generalist, Frontend Engineer, and the rest) and the 4 starter workflows now live in their own GitHub repo, [Team-Work-Source](https://github.com/yanun0323/Team-Work-Source). The app downloads them the first time it opens and keeps them up to date on its own, so they can get better without waiting for a new app version. After the first download, they work offline. They also come in English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

**Heads-up if you're upgrading**
Employees still set up on the old built-in blueprints keep working on your screen, but they're marked "blueprint no longer exists". Before giving them more work, open the employee, pick a blueprint again and save. If you skip this, a task that reaches them will stop and ask you to do it.

**Bigger images, smaller history**
Images you attach or paste can now be up to 25MB. Anything over 3.75MB is shrunk for you. Images are saved as separate files instead of inside the chat history, so long chats load lighter. Your old chats are moved over the first time you start the app. Nothing gets deleted.

**The camera follows someone when you step away**
Leave the map alone for a bit and the camera picks someone on the island and follows them around. Touch anything and it stops. You can choose how long it waits, or turn it off, in Settings. You can also let it follow employees inside their offices.

**People stop bumping into each other**
People on the island and in the offices now make way for each other. They step aside in narrow spots, wait at doors, and don't walk through anyone. Folks on the island also stop to watch planes fly by.

**A livelier airport**
The parked plane gets a proper visit from ground crew: trucks and equipment pull up while it waits. The planes themselves have more detail, too.

**Smaller touches**
- A new app icon.
- Groups in Settings can be folded, and the app remembers which ones you left open.
- When you scroll down a detail page, its buttons stay on top instead of text sliding under them.
- The map uses less power when the window isn't in focus.
