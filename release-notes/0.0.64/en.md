## What's new

**Your own profile and character**
Your face now sits at the top right of the map. Click it to see your milestones on this island: days in use, tasks finished, chats, how much you've said, tokens, cost, and the employee you work with most. Press Edit to dress your character piece by piece: hair, facial hair, an accessory, a top, what you wear below, shoes, and the colour of each, with any colour you like. Changes save as you make them. Your name is also what paired computers see, along with your character. The name field on the power tower is gone; click your name there to open your profile instead.

**You on your island**
Your character now walks around your own island with everyone else, and stays out at night. While your profile is open, the camera follows you, flying in, just panning, or staying put, as your Auto view setting says.

**Shoes**
People now wear shoes: leather shoes, sneakers, boots or flip-flops, or go barefoot. New employees get a random pair. Everyone already hired keeps the shoes they had.

**More of the map stays in view**
Opening a company, the airport, the power tower, the immigration office or your profile no longer hides the clock at the top left. It only steps aside when a page opens on the left, and comes back when that page closes. The help still clears the whole screen.

**No more tutorial**
The checklist beside the clock is gone, along with its settings and its sound. A new way to learn the app will come later.
