## What's new

**Employees can read your other chats**
Every chat has a copy button for its ID, like `chat-805`. Paste that ID into a message and the employee can open that chat and read the whole thing: who said what, and which tools they used. So you no longer have to copy and paste an old conversation to give someone the background. Long chats get read a piece at a time.

Employees only see chats from their own company. Airport staff can read chats from any company.
