## What's new

**Each model gets its own note colour**
In a chat, every reply now sits on a sticky note coloured by where its model comes from: Claude replies look one way, ChatGPT replies another, Gemini another. So you can tell at a glance who's talking to you. If two people in the same chat use the same kind of model, their notes get slightly different shades and a small name on top. The usage page uses the same colours, so a provider looks the same everywhere.

**Clicking the map closes pages first**
When you have a company or the airport open with a detail page on top, clicking the map now closes just that page first. Click again to close the company or airport. If you liked the old way, pick "Goes back to the main screen" under Settings → Clicking the map.

## Fixes

**Sharper shadows up close**
Shadows used to get blocky and lose thin bits, like bench slats, when you zoomed in on a big island. They now stay crisp at any zoom.
