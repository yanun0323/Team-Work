## What's new

**Your island builds public places as you use it**
Places appear on the island by themselves as you reach milestones: a park once 10 tasks are finished (it grows bigger at 50 and 200), a hill after 7 days of use, a fountain after 20 chats, a pier on the coast once you have 3 companies, and a clock tower after a year. The hill gets a stone lookout with a tablet and a telescope at 30 days, the fountain becomes a lake with a wooden bridge at 100 chats, and small boats moor at the pier at 5 companies. Once a place appears it stays where it is and only grows.

**Pathways, lamps and traffic lights**
Paved footpaths now run from every company's front door to the Immigration Office's, with lamp posts along them and, where paths cross, crosswalks, stop lines and traffic lights that change in turn. People walking about keep to the paths instead of cutting across the grass.

**People actually use these places**
The islanders sit on the benches, spread a picnic blanket, walk the loop round the park, climb the hill to look out, toss coins into the fountain, stand on the bridge watching the water, dangle their legs off the end of the pier with a fishing rod, and glance up at the clock. When they settle somewhere to sit or watch the view, they now stay a while, ten to twenty minutes, instead of getting up after a few seconds.

**You can move them**
Under Edit map, the public places and the Immigration Office can be dragged to a new spot like a company can, and both show a name tag so you can pick them. The pier always turns so its harbour faces open water, and no building may stand in the way boats come and go.

**Your own character wanders on its own**
With your profile open, your character goes about the island by itself until you press W, A, S or D (or hold the keys in the corner); stop for a moment and it wanders again. You walk a little faster than the islanders.

**Your landmark roads on your profile**
At the bottom of your profile, Island landmarks shows a road for each place, with a stop for everything it can become: what has grown shows its picture and name, what hasn't a question mark. Rest the pointer on a stop, or reach it with Tab, to see what it takes and how close you are. Click one that has grown and the camera flies there; click it again to follow yourself again.

## Fixes

**Offices are not crowded with visitors**
A company's office now shows only the 3 people from the Immigration Office who worked there most recently, instead of everyone from the last week. They walk about the room rather than taking a desk, so your own staff keep their seats.
