## What's new

**See what a step changed in a file**
When an employee hands over a file they changed, you no longer have to guess what's different. Open it and switch to "Unified" to see the old and new lines one above the other, or "Split" to see them side by side. Removed lines and new lines are marked, and long stretches that didn't change are folded away. The app remembers which view you like.

**A terminal at the airport**
The airport now has its own Terminal tab. It opens in your home folder, so you can run things that don't belong to any one company. It keeps running when you switch tabs, and airport chats can read what it shows, just like company chats read the company's terminal.

**Read a skill right from its source**
On a source's page, click any skill to open it next to the list and read the whole thing: what it's for, where the file is, and everything it says.

**Handing off a chat is easier to follow**
When you hand a chat to someone, the new copy opens right away and sits at the top of the list. It keeps the old chat's name with "Handoff:" in front, so you can tell them apart, and it doesn't stay pinned just because the old one was. Inside, the dashed line now says which chat it came from. Click it to open that chat.

**Meetings where employees think for themselves**
In a meeting, employees used to go along with whatever the first person said. Now they treat what others say as an opinion, check it themselves, and say so when they disagree.

## Smaller changes

- Pages you opened stay open when you switch tabs. Clicking the map closes those pages first; click again to close the company.
- File pages have a button next to the path that shows the file in Finder. SVG images show as pictures, and files that can't be shown just say so.
- On the task board, tasks that aren't done yet list the oldest first, so the ones waiting longest are on top.
- Detail pages now reach the bottom of the screen, and long files no longer get cut off at the edges.
- Workflow steps have shorter names: "Employee works" and "Meeting".
- Fixed a building that could vanish from the map if it came back right as it was going away.
