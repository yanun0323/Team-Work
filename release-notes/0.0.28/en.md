## What's new

**Copy a chat's ID in one click**
The chat's ID now shows at the bottom left of the message box. Click it and it's copied, so you can paste it wherever you need it.

**See how big your project is**
The company header now shows how much space the project takes up, right next to the branch name.

**Long choices don't push the chat away**
When an employee gives you a list of long options, the list now scrolls inside its own box. You can still see the conversation above it.

## Fixes

**Employees explain before they ask you to pick**
Sometimes an employee would just show you a few buttons with no real explanation. Now they have to write out their answer, what they suggest, and what they're asking before the buttons appear. Coworkers who pick up the chat later can see that explanation too.
