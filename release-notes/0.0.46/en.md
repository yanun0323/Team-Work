## What's new

**Employees can hire people and change their settings**
Ask an employee to bring someone new onto the team and they can do it themselves: pick a name, a blueprint and a model, and the new person joins right away. They can also hire several people at once.

If they want to change someone who's already on the team, like a new name, blueprint, model or thinking level, you'll see a card showing what would change, and nothing happens until you say yes. Changes for several people come on one card. If anything in the request doesn't work, like a model that doesn't exist, nothing is changed at all.

Employees can't let anyone go. That's still up to you.
