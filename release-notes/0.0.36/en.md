## What's new

**Long chats open fast**
A chat with a long history now opens with just the latest 100 messages. Want to see further back? Press "Show earlier messages" at the top and it loads 100 more, keeping your place. Nothing is deleted, it all stays saved.

**Less memory, less busywork**
The app no longer loads every chat's full history when it starts. It only reads a chat's history when that chat actually needs it, and lets go of it when done. The screen also stops redrawing everything once a second just because the clock ticked, so things stay calmer when you're not doing anything.

**A memory check for troubleshooting**
If the app ever seems to use too much memory, there's now an off-by-default switch that lets you grab a memory snapshot on your own computer. It's only reachable from your machine and only works when you turn it on.
