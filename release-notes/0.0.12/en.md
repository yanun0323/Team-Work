## What's new

**See your version at a glance**
- The app's version number now shows right under the clock in the top-left corner.

**Updating is one click away**
- When a new version is out, a small red **!** button shows up next to the time. Click it to update.
- The old update button in the Airport and the red dot on the Airport's sign on the map are gone. Everything lives by the clock now.
- You can still check any time from the menu with **Check for Updates…**.
