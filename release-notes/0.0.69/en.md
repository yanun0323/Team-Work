## What's new

**See what changed**
After an update, Team Work now shows what's new in that version, and you can read the notes for every version from About at the airport. The update window shows them too, before you install.

**Wider detail sheets**
A new setting, also a button in the corner of each sheet, spreads detail sheets over the whole company panel instead of just its left half. Click the panel underneath to put them away.

**Walk around with WASD**
While your profile is open and the camera follows you, walk your character with W, A, S and D, and hold Shift to run.

**The script tool can use every tool (experimental)**
The script tool, formerly the read-only script tool, can now call all of an employee's tools, not just the ones that only read, so one script can carry out a whole series of steps. Tools that wait for your answer or end a step are still left out.

**Context size from your own server**
For models on vLLM, SGLang, OpenRouter, llama.cpp or LM Studio, Team Work now uses the context length the server actually runs, so long conversations are shortened at the right time.

**Employees keep their memory when they move**
Copying an employee brings their memory along, and moving one into or out of the immigration office keeps their work and chats.

**Debug strip in settings**
A switch in the airport's settings shows the frame rate strip in the app.

**Earlier messages load as you scroll**
Scroll to the top of a chat and earlier messages appear by themselves, with no button to press.

**Official blueprints renamed and ordered**
DevOps Engineer is now SRE Engineer, and Matt Pocock is now General-purpose Engineer. The official blueprints and task flows now appear in a set order.

**Commits are yours**
Commits employees make in your project now use your own git name and email and leave no sign of the app. If git doesn't know who you are, the employee asks you to set it up.

**Smoother opening of companies**
Opening a company with many chats, and panels sliding in and out, take less work, especially on large screens.

## Fixes

**Updates install the newest version**
Team Work no longer installs an older update it downloaded in the background, which made you update again right after.

**Owner-led tasks no longer stall**
A task led by its owner could get stuck after a step was sent back. It now carries on.

**Sea ripples stay smooth**
After the app had been open for a few hours, the ripples on the sea turned into blocky patterns. They stay smooth now.

**Thinking level shown once**
"Default" no longer shows twice in the thinking level list, and settings saved with it load again.

**New items shown in the right place**
In a chat, what an employee creates now shows under the step that saved it, not the one where it only looked up the format.
