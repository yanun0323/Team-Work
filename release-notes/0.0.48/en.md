## What's new

**Model lists stay up to date**
The app now checks each provider for its latest models every time it starts, and again when you open a model list that hasn't been refreshed in a while. A provider's page also has a new button to reload its models whenever you want, so a model that came out today shows up without restarting.

**Any step can send work back**
You no longer need to turn on "Can send back" for each step. If a step gets work from just one step, and that step hands its work only to it, it can now always send the work back to be redone. The switch is gone from the flow editor. Flows you already have keep working.

**The island runs much faster**
The island now draws its buildings and people with far less work. When you zoom out, people are drawn in a simpler way that you can't tell apart from that distance. The map feels much smoother, especially when zoomed out with a lot of companies on it.
