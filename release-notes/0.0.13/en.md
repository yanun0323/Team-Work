## What's new

**See what one day used**
- In Usage, point at any day's bar and the table below switches to that day's numbers: tokens, cache hit rate and cost for each provider and model. Move away and it goes back to the full period.
- Bars are now always colored by model, so the "split by provider or model" switch is gone.

**Longer sunrise and sunset**
- Dawn and dusk on the island now last longer, and each stays at its most colorful for a full hour instead of flashing by.
