## What's new

**Meetings happen right in the task's conversation**
When the owner of a task calls a meeting, or the flow reaches a discussion step, the meeting now plays out in the task's conversation with the owner chairing. A bar above the message box shows the topic, who's in it, and a button to end it. You can join in, and use @ to pick who answers. To keep costs in check, the meeting pauses every so often and waits for you to let it go another round. Once it's over, it folds into a single card showing the conclusion, which you can open to read the whole meeting.

**Skip a question**
When an employee asks you to pick an option in a chat, you can now press Skip to dismiss the question without answering. The waiting mark goes away, and the employee isn't woken up just for that.

## Fixes

- When a task's owner finished the work and then said nothing more, the task could show the owner as failed. It doesn't anymore.
- Pressing Esc while writing a message leaves the message box and keeps your draft, without closing the panel.
- Speaking up in a meeting, or other steps moving on, no longer wipes out the words a participant is in the middle of saying.
