## What's new

**A sound switch for each building**
The Opening buildings switch in Settings is now four: Opening a company, the airport, the cell tower and the immigration office. Turn each on or off on its own, and press ▶ to hear it.

**The immigration office stamps when it opens**
Opening the immigration office used to be silent. Now a rubber stamp comes down on the desk with a soft thump, followed by a little rustle of paper.
