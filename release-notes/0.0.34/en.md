## What's new

**Six new building styles**
When you start a new company you can now get a lighthouse, a beach hut, a castle, a canal house, a glasshouse or a sweets shop. Companies you already have keep the building they've got.

**More things in the office**
Offices can now have a standing table, a tatami tea room, a foosball table, a game corner or a small gym.

**People have more to wear**
New hires come with lots of new hairstyles, skirts and dresses, more tops, and hats like straw hats, berets, bandanas, hair clips and flowers. A few even show up in swimwear. Everyone you hired before looks exactly the same as they did.

**See every look in the help**
The help now has a "Looks" section at the bottom with pictures of every building, every piece of office furniture and every outfit piece. Click one to see it bigger.
