## What's new

**Control who can do what on your island**
Computers you pair with can now only look by default. You choose for each one whether it may also make changes. Your tower lists who is visiting and what they have open, and their buildings are outlined on the map. You can send a visitor away or block them for an hour or until you lift it. You can also set the name other computers see for you.

**Claude signs in inside Team Work**
Claude now has its own sign-in in Team Work, separate from the `claude` command in your terminal. Press "Sign in to Claude" and finish in the browser. If the sign-in has expired, Team Work notices at startup and asks you to sign in again before an employee runs into it.

**Long tasks keep going**
When a task step or the task's owner builds up more conversation than the model can read, the earlier part is summed up and the work carries on instead of failing.

**Faster and cheaper model calls**
Claude employees pick up where they left off instead of starting over each time, and ChatGPT and OpenAI keys reuse more of what they already read. Replies come back sooner and use fewer tokens. A reply that goes quiet for five minutes is stopped and tried again instead of hanging.

**See an employee's blueprint on their page**
An employee's page now shows the blueprint they work from, and you can open it from there.

## Fixes

- Scrolling up in a chat, even a little, now stops it from jumping back to the bottom as new messages arrive. The "latest" button scrolls back down smoothly.
- A task card shows its progress on its own line, above the token count.
- Stopping a task while it was setting up its branch no longer leaves the branch broken.
