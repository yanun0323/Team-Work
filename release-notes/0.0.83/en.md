## What's new

**Shared employees show up in the offices they work in**
Employees from the Immigration Office who chatted or worked in a company over the last 7 days now get a desk and a name tag in its office, after the company's own staff. Their screens light up only when they are working on that company.

## Fixes

**Chats in a friend's company stay read**
Chats you opened in a friend's company on your island used to keep showing as unread on its name tag and the Dock. Now they stay read until a new reply comes. If your friend lets you operate, their unread mark clears too, so your own two Macs stay in step.

**Tidier New company sheet**
Switching between a project folder and a friend's company no longer leaves a gap in the sheet, and when a friend's company can't be picked yet, the reason shows right next to the choice.
