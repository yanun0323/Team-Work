## What's new

**Get a heads-up when something needs you**
- When the app is in the background and a task or chat is waiting on you, you'll get a Mac notification.
- The Dock icon shows how many things are waiting.
- Click a notification and the app jumps straight to that task or chat.
- Don't want them? Turn off "System notifications" in Settings.

**Easier to see when a chat was summed up**
- When an older part of a chat gets summed up to save room, you'll now see a simple line saying so, instead of a confusing technical message.
