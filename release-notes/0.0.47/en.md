## What's new

**Swap someone off a step right away**
When you edit a task and take someone off a step they're already working on, you now get two buttons instead of one. "Save, from the next step on" lets them finish what they started. "Save and swap now" stops them and hands the step to the new person, who picks up right where things left off. The step's log notes who replaced whom. If you didn't change anyone mid-step, you just see the usual Save.

**Smoother island on 120Hz screens**
On a ProMotion Mac, the island now moves at the screen's full speed instead of being stuck at 60 frames a second. When the window isn't in focus it still slows way down to save power.

## Fixes

**Sent-back work goes to the right person**
If you moved a step to someone else and that step later got sent back, the old person used to redo it. Now whoever is on the step at that point does the redo.
